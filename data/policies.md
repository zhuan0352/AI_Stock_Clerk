# Stock Clerk Hardware & Home — Customer Policies

## Returns
- Most items can be returned within **30 days** of delivery or in-store purchase.
- Items must be unused, in resalable condition, and in their original packaging.
- A receipt or order number is required. Online orders can be looked up with the email used at checkout.
- To start a return, contact support with your order number. We email a prepaid return label for online orders, or you can bring the item to any store.
- Return shipping is free for damaged, defective, or incorrect items. For other returns, a flat $5.99 return shipping fee is deducted from the refund. In-store returns are always free.

## Refunds
- Refunds go back to the **original payment method**.
- We issue the refund within 2 business days of receiving and inspecting the return. Banks usually take another **5–7 business days** to post it.
- Returns without a receipt are refunded as store credit at the item's current price.
- Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the return is due to our error or a defective item.
- Gift returns are refunded as store credit to the gift recipient.

## Exchanges
- Exchanges for a different size, color, or model are free within 30 days.
- If the replacement costs more, you pay the difference; if it costs less, we refund the difference.

## Items that cannot be returned
- Opened paint, caulk, glue, and other adhesives or sealants.
- Opened packages of batteries and light bulbs (unless defective).
- Custom-cut items (cut keys, cut-to-length chain, rope, or wire).
- Items marked "Final Sale" or "Clearance".
- Gift cards.

## Damaged, defective, or wrong items
- If an item arrives damaged, or is not what you ordered, tell us within **7 days** of delivery and include a photo if you can.
- If an item stops working or turns out to be defective at any time within the **30-day** return window, it can be returned even though it has been used.
- In both cases you choose a free replacement or a full refund, including original shipping, and return shipping is free.
- After 30 days, defects are handled under the warranty below.
- You do not need to send back items under $15; we will simply replace or refund them.

## Warranty
- Power tools (for example the cordless drill) carry a **1-year** limited manufacturer warranty against defects.
- Hand tools carry a lifetime warranty against breakage in normal use: bring the broken tool to a store for a free replacement.
- Warranty does not cover normal wear, misuse, or lost parts.

## Shipping and delivery
- Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days and costs $5.99. It is **free on orders over $35**.
- Expedited 2-day shipping costs $12.99.
- Free in-store pickup is usually ready within 2 hours.
- We currently ship only within the United States.

## Order changes and cancellations
- Orders can be changed or cancelled within **1 hour** of being placed.
- After that the order is already being packed; you can refuse delivery or return it under the normal return policy.

## Price matching
- If you find a lower advertised price on an identical in-stock item from a major retailer within 14 days of purchase, we refund the difference. Marketplace sellers and clearance prices are excluded.
